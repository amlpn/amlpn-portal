Atria Medical Laboratory Professionals Network Ltd (AMLPN) is a Ugandan-registered professional network uniting Medical Laboratory Professionals and stakeholders to strengthen the medical laboratory sector, empower our members, and improve health service delivery across Uganda.

👥
Who We Are
Learn more about AMLPN
▾

AMLPN is a professional network of Medical Laboratory Professionals and like-minded stakeholders drawn from districts across Uganda. We exist to promote excellence, integrity, and professionalism in medical laboratory practice — while also improving the social and economic wellbeing of our members.

We recognise the Uganda Medical Laboratory Technology Professionals Association (UMLTA) as the national professional body for medical laboratory professionals in Uganda, and we operate in full cooperation with UMLTA and the Allied Health Professionals Council.

🎯
Our Mission & Vision
What drives us forward
▾
Our Vision
To see that Medical Laboratory Professionals have a developed laboratory mind, act as an example to others, and equally enjoy the benefits of the profession.
Our Mission
To make Medical Laboratory Services more affordable, improve the integrity of Medical Laboratory Professionals, and enhance their livelihoods through wealth creation action plans.
🎓
Our Objectives
10 objectives that guide everything we do
▾
  1. To promote and improve health service delivery within the medical laboratory sector across Uganda.
  2. To promote self-esteem and build professional confidence among Medical Laboratory Professionals.
  3. To improve the livelihoods of Medical Laboratory Professionals through strategic wealth creation initiatives.
  4. To establish and implement development projects among members of the Network to foster job creation opportunities.
  5. To encourage and cultivate a culture of saving among the members of the Network.
  6. To promote, facilitate, and support scientific research among members of the Network.
  7. To organize professional seminars, activities, and forums that empower medical laboratory professionals to realize their full potential in the medical sector.
  8. To cooperate and collaborate with other professional bodies, including the Uganda Medical Laboratory Technology Professionals Association (UMLTA), to advance the interests of Medical Laboratory Professionals.
  9. To advocate for the rights, occupational welfare, and general interests of Medical Laboratory Professionals.
  10. To promote ethical practice, integrity, and strict professionalism within the medical laboratory sector.
